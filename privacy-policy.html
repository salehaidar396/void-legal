VOID — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-15

What VOID Is

VOID is a social music sharing app where you pick one song each day to share with friends. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, why, and what we do with it.

The Short Version

  • We collect what you'd expect: your account info, the songs you submit, and your reactions to friends' songs.
  • We use anonymized, aggregate music trend data (like "the top 10 most-picked songs this week") to help music industry professionals understand what people are actually listening to. This is how VOID stays free.
  • We never sell your personal information. Ever. No names, no emails, no listening habits tied to you.
  • We don't run ads.
  • Data selling is not currently active. When we reach sufficient scale (~10,000 users), we may begin selling aggregate, de-identified trend reports. You can opt out of this at any time.

Data We Collect

Account Information

When you sign up, we collect your email address, username, and display name. We use this to authenticate you and let your friends find you.

Submissions

When you submit your song of the day, we store the song title, artist, album, Spotify track ID, ISRC (a standard music identifier), your chosen snippet timestamps, and any photo you attach. This data is visible to your friends on VOID. If you turn on Discover, your songs are also visible to the people Discover matches you with (see below).

Reactions

When you react to a friend's submission (love or not for me), we store that reaction linked to the submission.

Streaming Service Connections

If you connect a streaming service (like Spotify), we store your access token and refresh token to search for songs on your behalf. We never access your streaming history or playlists.

Anonymous Analytics Events

When you submit a song or react to one, we log an anonymous event that contains only the song metadata (title, artist, album, Spotify track ID) and the event type. These events contain no user IDs, no email addresses, and no personally identifiable information. They cannot be traced back to you.

Push Notification Tokens

If you enable notifications, we store your device's push token to send you alerts when the daily window opens.

Discover (only if you turn it on)

Discover is off unless you turn it on. When it is on:

  • We compare the artists in the songs you post and the songs you love, plus your profile prompt answers, with other people who have also turned Discover on. Each day you see a few people with the most overlap, and the artists you have in common.
  • It works both ways: while you are in Discover, the people matched with you can see your username, display name, profile colours, prompt answers and songs. They cannot see your friends list or your email.
  • Your photos stay friends-only unless you also turn on "Also show my photos".
  • People matched with you can love your songs. Loves from Discover are anonymous: you see a count, not who it was.
  • We record when you turned Discover on. Turning it off in Settings → Privacy removes you from everyone's matches straight away, and your songs go back to friends-only.

Matching runs inside our database. It is not shared with anyone, not used for ads, and not part of any trend report. We rely on your consent for it (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time by turning Discover off.

How We Use Your Data

To Run the App

Your account info, submissions, reactions, and friend connections power the core VOID experience.

Anonymized Aggregate Trend Data

We analyze anonymous analytics events to produce aggregate music trend reports — things like:

  • "The 20 most-picked songs among VOID users this week"
  • "Artists appearing in 5%+ of daily submissions"
  • "Love-to-not-for-me ratios by song"

We may share or sell these aggregate, anonymized trend reports to music industry professionals (record labels, playlist curators, A&R teams). This data helps them understand what real people are genuinely excited about — not what an algorithm pushed.

Currently, data selling is disabled. We will only begin selling trend reports once we reach sufficient scale (~10,000 users) and will notify you in the app before this activates. Even after selling begins, you can opt out at any time from your Profile settings — your data will be excluded from all sold reports.

To be clear: we sell trend reports about songs and artists, not data about you. There is no way to identify any individual user from these reports.

What We Never Do

  • Never sell your personal information (email, username, listening habits tied to your identity)
  • Never share your data with advertisers
  • Never run ads in the app
  • Never access your streaming service history or playlists
  • Never track you across other apps or websites
  • Never collect your location, contacts, or browsing history

Data Retention

  • Your account data is kept as long as you have an account.
  • Anonymous analytics events are retained for up to 24 months, then automatically purged. They contain no personal information.
  • Streaming service tokens are kept until you disconnect the service or delete your account.
  • If you delete your account, all personal data is permanently deleted: account info, submissions, reactions, friend connections, push tokens, streaming tokens, and any photos you uploaded to the submission-photos storage bucket. Anonymous analytics events that were already logged remain, since they contain no information that could identify you.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Access your data by viewing your profile and submission history in the app.
  • Delete your account and all associated personal data at any time from the app settings.
  • Opt out of push notifications through your device settings.
  • Request a copy of your personal data by emailing us.
  • Opt out of data selling — If you live in California (CCPA) or simply prefer not to have your data included in aggregate trend reports, you can toggle the data-sale opt-out in your Profile settings. This ensures your analytics events are excluded from any sold reports.

If you're in the EU (GDPR) or California (CCPA), you have additional rights including the right to data portability, the right to know what data we've collected, and the right to request deletion. Contact us and we'll handle it within 30 days.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — hosts our database and authentication (their privacy policy: https://supabase.com/privacy)
  • Spotify API — used for song search only when you connect your account
  • Apple Push Notification Service — delivers push notifications to iOS devices
  • Odesli/song.link — generates cross-platform song links

Security

We use industry-standard security practices: encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords via Supabase Auth, and row-level security on our database. Analytics events are stored in a table that only our server can access — no client-side access is possible.

Children's Privacy

VOID is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a user is under 13, we will delete their account immediately. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll notify you through the app before they take effect.
